## Reading the findings

No series has a hard finding. The soft findings fall into four groups.

1. **One unadjusted split in a universe stock (fixed).** Yahoo lists the CCOLA 11-for-1
   bonus issue on 2024-08-13 but did not apply it to the price series: the close falls
   from 846.00 (2024-07-31) to 78.27 (2024-08-01), a false −90.7% day. The correction is
   recorded in `data/reference/price_adjustments.csv` and applied when a series is read;
   the stored snapshot is left as delivered. With the correction CCOLA has no price jump
   (the table above is computed on corrected data).
2. **Price jumps in five reference stocks (not fixed).** AYGAZ 2022-09-01 (+72.5%),
   TATGD 2024-12-18 (−44.3%), HEKTS 2021-04-30 (+44.1%) and 2024-09-09 (−64.8%),
   INFO 2020-11-24 (−60.8%), OSMEN 2024-08-01 (−94.2%). BIST limits a daily move to far
   less than 40%, so these are corporate actions the source handled wrongly, not price
   moves. Reference stocks are used only for a sector median over 5 to 60 days, so the
   rule is: **a member whose return window contains such a jump is left out of the
   median and named in the warnings** (`rank_sector_relative_strength`).
3. **Zero-volume days.** Almost every stock has one or two (single days where the source
   has a price but no volume). **SASA has 155, 128 of them in 2026**, while its price
   keeps changing on those days: the volume field is missing, the stock was trading.
   Volume-based features (volume regime, high-volume reactions) are therefore unreliable
   for SASA in 2025–2026; the Sprint 4 quality gate must attach this warning to any SASA
   analysis that uses volume.
4. **Missing days.** Most stocks lack 2024-04-09 (the index has a bar, the stocks do
   not). XU030, XBANK and XUSIN lack eleven days that XU100 has (2021–2024). XU100 is the
   trading calendar for all checks.
